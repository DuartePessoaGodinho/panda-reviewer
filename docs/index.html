The problem

MR review is
friction by default.

GitLab doesn't live in your periphery. Checking on MRs means switching to a browser, navigating to the right project, and losing whatever mental context you had. Small, constant interruptions — and the result is stale reviews, forgotten approvals, and your own MRs sitting idle.

Panda Reviewer removes the friction. It runs in the background and tells you exactly what needs attention, exactly when it needs attention.

01Notice MR activity
02Open a browser
03Navigate to GitLab
04Find the right project
05Lose your train of thought
→Click the tray icon. Done.
What it does

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Live MR dashboard
Grouped by priority — what needs your review and what's waiting on others. Always fresh, always one click away.
Diff viewer
Browse diffs directly in the app. No browser tab, no navigation. Syntax-highlighted, familiar format.
Open in IDE
One click opens the repo at the right branch in VS Code, IntelliJ, or whatever editor you've configured.
AI-assisted review
Run an AI review against your local branch. Get a structured summary before you even open the diff.
Approve without the browser
Review and approve MRs from the app itself. Zero GitLab tabs required.
Fully local, no telemetry
All data stays on your machine. No accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics. Your token goes nowhere except GitLab.
